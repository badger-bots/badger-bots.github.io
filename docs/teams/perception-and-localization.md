# Perception Team

**Team Lead**: Nikolas Margaritis

**Team Description**: Gives our robots the clearest picture of the game possible. The team owns perception (especially computer vision), localization, and the interface between the two.

## Vision

1. Robot detection
2. 3D ball perception (ball in air)
3. Line detection

## Localization

1. Off-field landmarks
2. Field lines used in localization
3. On-field calibration
4. Ball prediction (using ball velocity to estimate where ball is going)
5. Opponent locations
6. Memory of ball (when ball is lost)
